FAQ

Common questions

What information do you need for a quote?

Where it is collected from and going to, what the goods are, the weight or number of pallets, and when it is ready. For imports and exports, the commodity and its value help us check duty and paperwork early.

Do you handle customs clearance?

Yes. Customs clearance and the related documents are part of our import and export service, so your goods are not held at the port waiting for paperwork.

Can you help a small business that ships occasionally?

Yes. We work with businesses of all sizes, from a first shipment to regular monthly volumes.

Do you move household goods abroad?

Yes. Our relocation service covers homes and offices: packing, shipping and delivery at the other end.
