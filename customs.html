Do you handle customs clearance?

Yes. Customs clearance and the related documents are part of our import and export service, so your goods are not held at the port waiting for paperwork.

Do I need an EORI number?

UK businesses importing or exporting goods normally need one. We can tell you how to get it if you do not have one yet.
